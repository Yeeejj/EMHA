1. How do you know there is no leakage?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Splitting is by participant, never by crop. DATASET/metadata/folds.csv holds
one row per participant (src/training/splits.py, make_outer_folds), so all 24
crops of a person sit in one fold. Every runner calls
splits.assert_no_leakage on train, test, and middle-band IDs in every fold
(run_baselines.py, run_cnn.py, permutation.py; CrossValidator in
cross_validate.py also checks inner train and validation). run_hybrid._check_split
refuses a CNN checkpoint whose stored split differs. Scalers and PCA sit inside
sklearn Pipelines fitted on training participants only (run_baselines.fit_lr).
The evaluator rejects any participant predicted twice (evaluator.validate).
tests/test_splits.py tests these guards, e.g.
test_one_row_per_participant_no_participant_in_two_folds.

3. Why an HMM on offline images?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Offline scans lose pen timing, but writing still runs left to right. The
fine-tuned CNN's layer3 map, averaged over height, gives one feature vector
per image column; read left to right, these form a sequence per word or
cursive crop (CNNConfig.seq_layer; src/models/hybrid.py, crop_sequences). One
Gaussian HMM per class models how those column features change along the
line (src/models/hmm.py). Topology, state count, and PCA size are chosen on
inner-validation participants (HybridConfig; run_hybrid.select_hmm). Whether
the sequence model adds anything is tested, not assumed: the backbone
ablation compares the CNN head with the CNN-HMM (secondary.txt > 3.
Ablations) [PENDING]. Limitation: the HMMs see features of images the CNN
trained on (run_hybrid.py docstring; protocol section 5).

4. Are the self-report labels reliable?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The FINALE instrument was designed with 4 registered psychometricians
(CLAUDE.md; DOCS/FINALE Thesis Assessment.pdf). It has 24 Likert items, 12
reverse-scored (config.py, ReportConfig). Labels are read verbatim from the
scoring export and never recomputed (src/data/labeler.py). Internal
consistency is Cronbach's alpha with a bootstrap CI
(src/analysis/questionnaire_report.py) [PENDING: alpha value;
results/questionnaire/report.txt not yet generated]. The extreme-groups
design sets aside participants nearest the cutoff, where a label is least
certain (DOCS/decisions/analysis_design.txt). The labels remain concurrent
self-report, not diagnosis, and that is the stated scope
(DOCS/drafts/methods.txt, section 1).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Why no binarization or deskew?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The proposal planned both (README.md, Methodology). They were dropped before
any model was run because they delete candidate signal. Binarization removes
ink darkness, so stroke-intensity variation cannot be measured. Deskewing
removes baseline slope and slant, which are measured as features
(baseline_angle_deg, slant_deg in src/features/handcrafted.py). Stretch
resizing would distort size and aspect ratio. The pipeline does grayscale
background flattening, one fixed scale per family, and white padding only
(src/preprocessing/pipeline.py docstring; PreprocessingConfig). Otsu appears
only in descriptive statistics, never in a model input. Augmentation
excludes rotation, scaling, shear, and flips for the same reason
(AugmentConfig).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. How does this compare with online-tablet studies such as EMOTHAW?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EMOTHAW recorded 129 participants on a digitizing tablet, with pen position,
in-air movement, timing, and pressure, across seven tasks including house
drawing and cursive copying. Labels were DASS anxiety, depression, and
stress. Random forests reached 60% to 71% (CITATIONS/EMOTHAW_A_novel_database_
for_emotional_state_recog.pdf, abstract). This study uses offline 200 dpi
scans (IngestConfig) with no timing, in-air, or pressure channel, and a
binary HAPPY/SAD self-report. The numbers are therefore not directly
comparable: different labels, inputs, and samples. Our result [PENDING:
RESULTS.txt > PRIMARY RESULTS] shows what is recoverable from paper alone,
which matches how handwriting is usually collected.

7. Is it better than chance?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two checks, both fixed in advance (EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md section 4).
First, every accuracy is shown beside the majority baseline: the training-fold
majority label (src/training/evaluator.py, majority_hits). Second, a
permutation test shuffles training labels within each outer fold, refits the
whole pipeline, and scores test participants on their true labels;
p = (1 + #null >= observed) / (1 + N) (src/analysis/permutation.py). N is
1,000 for the two ensemble logistic regressions and 100 full retrains for
the CNN models and the ensemble (PermutationConfig). The test is checked on
synthetic data: not significant without signal, and the smallest attainable
p with signal (tests/test_permutation.py). Real values: [PENDING: RESULTS.txt
> PRIMARY RESULTS, permutation column].

8. Why set aside the middle band, and how is that not cherry-picking?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Participants near the cutoff carry the least certain labels. The band
fraction (0.20) was chosen on 2026-10-04 from labels.csv scores only, before
any model existed, as the smallest previewed fraction that kept at least 100
per class (DOCS/decisions/analysis_design.txt; commit 2bb867a). Membership
never depends on predictions (EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md section 1). The protocol
was approved on 2026-10-05 and is frozen by the git tag protocol-frozen;
runners refuse real data until the tag exists
(src/utils/protocol_guard.py). The full sample is reported alongside, and the
middle band is predicted as a secondary analysis (protocol section 6).
