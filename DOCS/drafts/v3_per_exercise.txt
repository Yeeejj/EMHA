PER-EXERCISE RESULTS (v3, EXPLORATORY): HOW TO PRESENT THEM (DRAFT)

Purpose. This draft shows how the thesis can report, exercise by exercise,
whether each of the 24 FINALE exercises identified the respondent's
self-reported state (HAPPY or SAD). Every number is copied from
DOCS/plans/v3_result.txt (verbatim blocks of results/final/EXPLORATORY_v3.txt).
Tables A and B were generated from that file by script, not typed.

Where it goes. Results chapter, after the primary result (v1) and the
exploratory v2 result, under a heading such as "Per-exercise analysis
(exploratory)". Methods: DOCS/drafts/methods.txt section 12.

================================================================================
1. LEAD PARAGRAPH (suggested text)
================================================================================

To see which exercises carry information about the self-reported state, each
of the 24 FINALE exercises was modelled on its own, with four models per
exercise, on the primary set of 320 respondents (174 HAPPY, 146 SAD). This
analysis was added after the main results were known and is exploratory
(Deviation 6). No single exercise identified the respondents' self-reported
state better than chance. Across the 96 exercise-and-model results,
macro-F1 ranged from 0.367 to 0.574, against a majority-class accuracy of
0.544. Five of the 48 logistic-regression tests had p < 0.05 before
correction, and none remained after correcting for the 24 exercises tested
per model (smallest q = 0.2478). The fused CNN-HMM over all 24 exercises,
the one test declared in advance, reached macro-F1 0.371 [0.337, 0.410]
with a permutation p of 1.0000 (N = 20).

4. TEXT PER TASK FAMILY (suggested; no ranking words)
================================================================================

Drawings (D1 circles, D2 dots, D3 "Draw a Person in the Rain", D4 "Draw a
House and a Tree"). Macro-F1 ranged from
0.500 to 0.547 for the handcrafted LR, 0.468 to 0.539 for the embedding LR,
0.485 to 0.550 for the CNN head and 0.397 to 0.482 for the CNN-HMM. The
handcrafted LR on D4 had p = 0.0370 before correction (q = 0.4436).

Words (content, melancholic, optimistic, disconnected, vibrant; each written
with the left hand, the right hand and in uppercase). Macro-F1 ranged from
0.453 to 0.559 for the handcrafted LR, 0.367 to 0.556 for the embedding LR,
0.437 to 0.551 for the CNN head and 0.404 to 0.514 for the CNN-HMM. Two
word results had p < 0.05 before correction: the handcrafted LR on W2_LH
(melancholic, left hand; p = 0.0240, q = 0.4436) and the embedding LR on
W3_LH (optimistic, left hand; p = 0.0180, q = 0.2478).

Cursive (passages CS1 to CS5). Macro-F1 ranged from 0.484 to 0.528 for the
handcrafted LR, 0.475 to 0.556 for the embedding LR, 0.460 to 0.574 for the
CNN head and 0.438 to 0.479 for the CNN-HMM. Two cursive results had
p < 0.05 before correction: the embedding LR on CS4 (p = 0.0260, q =
0.2478) and on CS5 (p = 0.0310, q = 0.2478).

Selected exercise. The model that picks one exercise per fold using only
inner-validation respondents (cnn_hmm_v3_selected) reached macro-F1 0.487
[0.431, 0.542]. It picked a different exercise in each of the 5 folds (CS1,
W2_RH, W1_UC, CS4, W3_UC), so no exercise was chosen consistently.

6. WORDING RULES FOR THIS SECTION
================================================================================

  - Say "no exercise identified the self-reported state better than chance
    after correction". Do not call any exercise "the best", "the strongest"
    or "the most predictive": with 24 exercises per model, some rows land
    high or low by chance.
  - Whenever an LR p below 0.05 is mentioned, give its q next to it.
  - Treat the rows with a CI lower bound above 0.5 (handcrafted LR on
    W2_LH, 0.559 [0.503, 0.615]; CNN head on CS4, 0.574 [0.519, 0.627]) as
    descriptive only; neither model on those exercises has a test that
    survives correction (the CNN head has no per-exercise test).
  - Write "associated with" or "predicted", never "detects", "reveals" or
    "diagnoses". The label is a concurrent self-report, not a diagnosis.
  - Keep v1 as the primary result; this section is exploratory.

================================================================================
7. ONE-SENTENCE VERSION (abstract, defense slide)
================================================================================

When each of the 24 FINALE exercises was modelled on its own, no exercise
identified respondents' self-reported happiness or sadness better than
chance after correcting for the number of exercises tested.

Source: DOCS/plans/v3_result.txt (sections 2 to 6).
