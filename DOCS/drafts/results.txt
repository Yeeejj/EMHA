================================================================================
PER-EXERCISE TRAINING (v3, EXPLORATORY, Deviation 6)
================================================================================

Every number in this section is copied from DOCS/plans/v3_result.txt, which
holds the blocks of results/final/EXPLORATORY_v3.txt verbatim. v3 is post
hoc and exploratory; it is not part of the primary claim, and v1 remains
the primary model.

Sample. The primary set held 320 participants (174 HAPPY, 146 SAD). In each
of the 5 folds, 217 participants trained each per-exercise model, 39 were
used for inner validation, and 64 were tested. The majority baseline
accuracy was 0.544.

Pre-declared test. The one test declared before any v3 result was the
fused CNN-HMM over all 24 exercises, cnn_hmm_v3_fused/all24. It reached
macro-F1 0.371 [0.337, 0.410], accuracy 0.534 [0.481, 0.591], balanced
accuracy 0.494 [0.473, 0.513] and ROC-AUC 0.536 [0.471, 0.602], against a
majority baseline of 0.544. The permutation test gave p = 1.0000 (N = 20,
seed 42), with a null mean of 0.467: the observed macro-F1 was lower than
every one of the 20 permuted values. Under the permutation test, its
macro-F1 was not distinguishable from what would be expected if
handwriting and label were unrelated.

Its macro-F1 was lower than each reference model on the same participants:
-0.144 [-0.206, -0.079] against lr_handcrafted/all, -0.063 [-0.118, -0.005]
against v1's cnn_hmm_fused/fused, -0.204 [-0.268, -0.139] against
lr_embeddings/concat, -0.163 [-0.227, -0.098] against cnn_head_fused/fused,
and -0.110 [-0.163, -0.053] against cnn_hmm_v2/task_order. All five CIs
excluded zero.

Other combinations (descriptive). The fused CNN head had macro-F1 0.500
[0.444, 0.554] over all 24 exercises, 0.510 [0.454, 0.562] over drawing,
0.476 [0.422, 0.532] over word, 0.506 [0.450, 0.563] over cursive and 0.501
[0.445, 0.555] over word and cursive. The fused CNN-HMM had 0.430 [0.382,
0.479] over drawing, 0.375 [0.337, 0.415] over word, 0.438 [0.386, 0.491]
over cursive and 0.379 [0.342, 0.420] over word and cursive. The
inner-validation-selected model, cnn_hmm_v3_selected, had macro-F1 0.487
[0.431, 0.542] and ROC-AUC 0.469 [0.404, 0.530]. The exercise it used
differed in every fold (CS1, W2_RH, W1_UC, CS4 and W3_UC).

Compared with the v1 model of the same kind and family, each v3 family row
had a macro-F1 difference whose CI included zero (drawing, word and cursive
for the CNN head; word and cursive for the CNN-HMM). For word_cursive
against v1's fused CNN-HMM the difference was -0.055 [-0.110, -0.000]; the
upper bound rounds to zero as printed.

Per exercise (descriptive). Table V3 lists macro-F1 [CI] for the four
models on each of the 24 exercises (v3_result.txt, "4. Per exercise"). Across
the 24 exercises, macro-F1 ranged from 0.453 to 0.559 for lr_handcrafted_v3,
0.367 to 0.556 for lr_embeddings_v3, 0.437 to 0.574 for cnn_head_v3 and
0.397 to 0.514 for cnn_hmm_v3. These rows are descriptive. With 24
exercises per model, some rows are expected to land high or low by chance,
and no single row is reported as a finding.

LR permutation tests. Each per-exercise LR model was tested with N = 1,000
permutations, and the 24 p-values of each model were corrected with
Benjamini-Hochberg (q = 0.05). Five of the 48 tests had p < 0.05 before
correction: lr_handcrafted_v3 on D4 (p = 0.0370, q = 0.4436) and W2_LH
(p = 0.0240, q = 0.4436), and lr_embeddings_v3 on W3_LH (p = 0.0180,
q = 0.2478), CS4 (p = 0.0260, q = 0.2478) and CS5 (p = 0.0310, q = 0.2478).
No test had q at or below 0.05.

Reading the fused CNN-HMM. A balanced accuracy of 0.494 and a macro-F1 of
0.371 next to an accuracy of 0.534 point to a model that predicted mostly
one class; the confusion matrix (results/per_task_v3/primary/confusion.csv)
gives the exact split. Accuracy alone would hide this, which is why
macro-F1 and balanced accuracy are reported next to it.

Figure note. In fig_v3_per_task_forest.png the dashed "majority baseline"
line is at 0.544, the majority-class accuracy, while the x-axis is
macro-F1. The caption must say this. A classifier that always predicts
HAPPY has a macro-F1 of about 0.35, not 0.544.

Limitations. Each per-exercise model trained on about 217 images, far fewer
than the v1 family models. The same 39 inner-validation participants per
fold were used for CNN early stopping, HMM selection, Platt calibration and
the selected exercise. The whole v3 design was set after v1 and v2 results
were known.
