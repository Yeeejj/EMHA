DEFENSE OUTLINE (DRAFT, 15 slides)
Numbers are copied exactly from results/final/RESULTS.txt (git HEAD 996340d)
and results/final/secondary.txt. Primary set: n = 320, pooled out-of-fold,
95% bootstrap CIs (2,000 participant resamples). Do not round or recompute.

1. Title
   - Emotion classification (HAPPY vs SAD) from offline handwriting and
     drawings with a CNN-HMM hybrid. Author, adviser, date.

2. Problem
   - Can a scanned page of handwriting and drawings indicate a concurrent
     self-reported emotional state? Offline scans lose pen timing and
     pressure, unlike tablet studies such as EMOTHAW.
   - Self-reported state, not a diagnosis; associations, not causes.

3. FINALE instrument and dataset
   - 400 right-handed USC students aged 18-25; 24 crops each (4 drawings,
     15 word cells, 5 cursive sentences); 9,600 crops.
   - Designed with 4 registered psychometricians; 24-item Likert self-report.
   - Cronbach's alpha 0.8803 [0.8562, 0.8994] (results/questionnaire/report.txt).

4. Labeling and extreme groups
   - Labels read verbatim from the export: HAPPY 224, SAD 176 (cutoff 72).
   - Middle band (fraction 0.20, 80 participants, scores 70-74) set aside;
     primary set 320 (HAPPY 174, SAD 146). Figure 5.1.

5. Pipeline
   - Fixed-coordinate crops; grayscale, background flattening, median
     denoise, fixed-factor scale and white pad; no binarization, deskew or
     stretch (Figure 3). Participant-level 5-fold CV (folds.csv).

6. CNN-HMM v1 (PRIMARY MODEL)
   - Fine-tuned ResNet18 layer3 -> height-averaged column sequence ->
     standardize + PCA -> per-class Gaussian HMMs -> length-normalized
     log-likelihood difference -> Platt -> P(SAD), averaged per participant
     (Figure 4). Primary model per Deviation 4 (protocol section 9).

7. Baselines and supporting ensemble
   - Majority class; logistic regression on handcrafted features and on
     frozen ResNet18 embeddings; fine-tuned ResNet18 head.
   - Fixed equal-weight ensemble (LR handcrafted, LR embeddings, CNN-HMM):
     named primary in the frozen protocol, reported as supporting.

8. Protocol and leakage safeguards
   - Protocol frozen before any real result (tag protocol-frozen, 918990a);
     deviations 1-5 logged with what had been seen.
   - Participant-level splits, assert_no_leakage in every fold; scalers,
     PCA, HMM selection and Platt fit on training participants only;
     threshold fixed at 0.5; every model reported.

10. Permutation tests and CIs
   - p = 0.7143 (N = 20) CNN-HMM; 0.4286 (N = 20) ensemble; 0.1905 (N = 20)
     CNN head; 0.2827 (N = 1000) LR handcrafted; 0.0080 (N = 1000) LR
     embeddings.
   - Paired macro-F1 vs lr_handcrafted/all: CNN-HMM -0.081 [-0.157, +0.001];
     LR embeddings +0.060 [-0.006, +0.128]; ensemble +0.001 [-0.048, +0.052].

11. Why the CNN-HMM did not separate the classes
   - Balanced accuracy 0.501 [0.465, 0.537], ROC-AUC 0.485 [0.422, 0.550]:
     predicted HAPPY for 281 of 320 (hybrid/primary/predictions.csv).
   - Ablation (secondary.txt section 3): CNN head minus CNN-HMM
     +0.101 [+0.019, +0.180]; the same CNN features carried more signal
     without the HMM over image columns.
   - The same frozen features averaged per participant (LR embeddings)
     gave p = 0.0080.

   - Idea: one HMM sequence per respondent, the 24 task images in FINALE
     form order, frozen ResNet18 features; designed after results were seen,
     never part of the primary claim.

13. Limitations
   - One site, one population (right-handed students 18-25); self-report
     labels; extreme-groups result applies away from the cutoff only.
   - Offline scans: no timing or pressure. 20 CNN permutations (smallest
     p = 0.0476). Primary model changed after results (Deviation 4).

14. Conclusions and recommendations
   - Under this protocol, no model reached the targets; only LR on frozen
     embeddings differed from the permutation null (p = 0.0080).
   - Recommendations: online pen data, full score range, a second site,
     separate calibration and selection sets.
