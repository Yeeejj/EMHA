fig1_conceptual_framework: Conceptual framework (input-process-output): self-report responses and scanned FINALE pages are labeled, cropped and preprocessed, and classified by the CNN-HMM v1 (primary model), with logistic-regression baselines and a fixed supporting ensemble alongside, under participant-level cross-validated evaluation.
fig3_data_flow: Data flow from scan to model input, as implemented in src/preprocessing/pipeline.py: fixed-coordinate crop, grayscale, background flattening, 3 x 3 median denoise, fixed-factor scale and white padding per task family, inversion, and joining of labels by participant.
fig4_cnn_hmm_architecture: CNN-HMM v1 architecture: layer3 features of the fine-tuned ResNet18 are averaged over height into a left-to-right column sequence, standardized and PCA-reduced, scored by per-class Gaussian HMMs, Platt-calibrated, and averaged per family and then across word and cursive families into a participant P(SAD).
fig5_kanban: Project kanban board built from the stages recorded in the main-branch git history (as of 2026-10-23).
fig6_bottom_up: Bottom-up integration: units from labeling to evaluation were built and tested in order, then passed the smoke-test, pilot and full-run gates before results were reported.
fig_5_1_score_hist: Distribution of adjusted total scores for all 400 participants, with the HAPPY/SAD cutoff (72; SAD below 72) and the middle band (scores 70-74, 80 participants; ties at the band edges split by the labeler's stable order) set aside from the primary set.
